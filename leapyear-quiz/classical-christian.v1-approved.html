What you'll walk away with

In 45 minutes, you'll leave with:

A clearer picture of what your senior needs now

Beyond the college decision — what's actually at stake in the months between graduation and launch, and how to think about it clearly.

A framework for launching well

The three things every 18-year-old has to develop before launching well: career clarity, leadership capacity, and spiritual formation.

Answers to your specific questions

About LeapYear, the cohort, the rhythm, and whether it might be the right next step for your family.

Who it's for

Who this webinar is for

You've given your student a classical Christian education because you believe formation matters more than credentials. You've invested in critical thinking, creative problem-solving, civil discourse, and a rooted faith. Now your senior is approaching a decision that most 18-year-olds aren't ready to make, in a world that's changing faster than any generation before them.

This webinar is for parents who want to think clearly about what their senior needs in this moment, and who are open to the idea that the year after high school might be the most formative year of their student's life.

About your host

Riley Simpson, Founder of LeapYear.

Riley is the Founder of LeapYear, a 9-month leadership incubator in Austin, Texas for recent high school graduates. A Classical Conversations alum (Challenge B), Riley took two gap years before college, studied entrepreneurship and theology at Abilene Christian University, and worked in education venture capital before founding LeapYear.

The program's founding cohort includes students who deferred offers from Stanford, Purdue, and Texas A&M.

“LeapYear helped my application to St. John's College stand out, and my coach was instrumental in helping me secure an 80% scholarship for all four years. I came into LeapYear without a plan for college, and I've narrowed down my personal curiosities and orientation to a career.”
— Dominic Johnson,
LeapYear Founding Cohort
